Yes.

Interpreting “prime factors of an integer” as the primes dividing its absolute value, take
[
x=-4,\qquad y=2.
]

* (x=-4) and $y=2$ have prime factors $\\{2\\}$ [[nomath]](since $|-4|=2^2$ and $2$ is prime)[[/nomath]].
* (x+1=-3) and (y+1=3) have prime factors $\\{3\\}$.
* (x+2=-2) and (y+2=4) have prime factors $\\{2\\}$ [[nomath]](since $|-2|=2$ and $4=2^2$)[[/nomath]].

So ((x,y)=(-4,2)) works (and of course swapping them also works).
