Let

[
S=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{p_n}{2^n}\qquad(p_n=\text{the }n\text{th prime}).
]

As of today (January 2026), **it is not known** whether $S$ is rational or irrational.

This question was raised by Erdős, and he **conjectured** that this kind of series should be irrational [[nomath]](more generally $\sum p_n^k/2^n$ for each fixed $k\ge1$)[[/nomath]], but he did not have a proof even for (k=1). 

It is also listed as an **open Erdős problem** (Erdős Problem #251). ([erdosproblems.com][1])

If you want to see the numerical value, its decimal expansion is recorded in OEIS as **A098990**. ([oeis.org][2])
